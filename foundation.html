Community Stewardship

What community initiatives are driven by the Bongshai Foundation?

Bongshai Foundation (bongshaifoundation.org) is the social welfare organization of Bongshai Group. We invest in long-term human development through educational grants, medical assistance, and emergency response logistics.

Funded through an annual allocation of corporate profits, the Foundation works directly with local community leaders, schools, and hospitals to build sustainable infrastructure and provide direct relief to families in need.

Key Welfare Pillars

How does Bongshai Foundation distribute support?

Structured programs designed to achieve lasting, quantifiable community benefits.

Academic Scholarships

Direct financial aid for STEM undergraduate students, polytechnic diplomas, and vocational technical certifications.

Rural Health Camps

Free medical check-ups, eye surgeries, essential medicines, and maternal healthcare services for vulnerable populations.

Disaster & Flood Relief

Rapid distribution of emergency dry rations, clean drinking water, temporary shelters, and post-flood home rebuilding.
