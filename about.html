Corporate Heritage

What is the story and mission behind Bongshai Group?

Founded in 2008 by Engineer S. M. Awal, Bongshai Group (bongshai.com) is an engineering-driven conglomerate committed to constructing resilient mega-structures, manufacturing certified steel systems, and fostering national socio-economic prosperity.

From our headquarters in Uttara, Dhaka, we manage multi-million-dollar EPC&C contracts, large-scale industrial factory sheds, and premium residential projects. Our integrated supply chains ensure seamless material availability, zero compromise on quality, and strict adherence to BNBC and ISO standards.

Guiding Principles

What values guide Bongshai Group's corporate decisions?

Our foundation rests on uncompromising technical precision, safety, and accountability.

Chief Executive

Engr. S. M. Awal

Chief Executive Officer & Chief Structural Engineer

"Infrastructure is not just concrete and steel; it is the physical framework that allows society and commerce to thrive."

Engineer S. M. Awal founded Bongshai Group with the conviction that domestic engineering firms can execute world-standard mega projects. Under his leadership, the group has expanded to over 500 successfully delivered installations across Bangladesh.

How does corporate governance ensure quality at Bongshai Group?

Every subsidiary company maintains an independent technical committee adhering to central Group quality benchmarks.

  • Certified Materials: We source only tested structural steel and high-durability aggregates.
  • Modern BIM & Structural Modeling: Advanced finite element structural simulations prior to breaking ground.
  • Third-Party Quality Verification: Regular testing through accredited national engineering laboratories.
