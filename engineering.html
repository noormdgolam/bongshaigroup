EPC&C Capabilities

What engineering solutions does Bongshai Engineering deliver?

Bongshai Engineering (bongshaiengineering.com) is the core EPC&C contracting subsidiary of Bongshai Group. We manage full lifecycle engineering, from architectural design to multi-story structural erection and mechanical commissioning.

Equipped with advanced hydraulic pile-driving machinery, tower cranes, and a seasoned team of structural engineers led by CEO S. M. Awal, Bongshai Engineering has successfully completed over 200 civil and industrial contracts with zero critical safety incidents.
