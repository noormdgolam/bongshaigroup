Corporate Vision

What is the vision and strategy of Bongshai Group?

Headquartered in Dhaka, Bongshai Group serves as an infrastructural backbone for major national and regional growth. By integrating design, manufacturing, engineering, and digital logistics, we eliminate supply chain bottlenecks and guarantee unmatched precision.

  • Turnkey EPC&C Execution: Full-lifecycle engineering, procurement, construction, and commissioning.
  • Heavy Steel Fabrication: Certified pre-engineered buildings (PEB) and industrial manufacturing plants.
  • Sustainable Real Estate: Energy-efficient housing, commercial complexes, and modern modular spaces.

Who governs Bongshai Group's engineering quality and standards?

Bongshai Group operates under strict governance frameworks audited by certified quality engineers and structural consultants.

  • Rigorous ISO 9001:2015 Processes: Continuous internal auditing and raw material testing.
  • AISC 360 Structural Compliance: American Institute of Steel Construction design codes for heavy frameworks.
  • Environmental & Sustainability Safeguards: Zero-waste fabrication techniques and energy-efficient building blueprints.
ESG & Social Impact

How does Bongshai Group create community and environmental impact?

Through the Bongshai Foundation and our green engineering practices, we channel corporate capabilities into empowering marginalized communities, building flood-resilient infrastructure, and granting higher education STEM scholarships across Bangladesh.

  • 1,200+ Scholarships: Supporting future engineers, architects, and polytechnic apprentices.
  • 85+ Healthcare Camps: Mobile clinical teams delivering maternal health and safe water filtration.
  • 100% Green Steel Circularity: Industrial scrap recycling and solar-integrated modular architecture.
