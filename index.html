What is the vision of Bongshai Group?

Driving Progress Through EPC&C Excellence

Established in 2008 in Dhaka, Bangladesh, Bongshai Group's vision is to be the cornerstone of infrastructural development. From complex civil engineering and high-rise construction to premium real estate and pre-engineered steel buildings (PEB), we deliver holistic, world-class solutions.

  • Premier EPC&C Contracting
  • Pre-Engineered Steel Buildings (PEB)
  • BNBC 2020 & AISC 360 Compliant
