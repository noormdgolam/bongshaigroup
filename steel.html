Heavy Fabrication

How does Bongshai Steel power industrial infrastructure?

Bongshai Steel (bongshaisteel.com) is the specialized industrial manufacturing wing of Bongshai Group. We produce high-tensile steel framing, PEB superstructures, and modular container units engineered to withstand severe seismic and wind loads.

Equipped with CNC automatic plasma cutting tables, submerged arc welders, and shot-blasting priming chambers, our manufacturing plant has a monthly capacity exceeding 2,500 metric tons, serving textile mills, pharmaceutical facilities, and mega logistics hubs.
