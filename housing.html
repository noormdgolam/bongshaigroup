What makes Bongshai Housing a market leader in real estate?

Bongshai Housing (bongshaihousing.com) is the premier real estate arm of Bongshai Group. We blend luxury architectural aesthetics with seismic-resistant engineering and modern modular prefabrication.

Our residential condominiums and duplex villas feature smart-home automation, rooftop gardens, energy-efficient HVAC zoning, and 100% legal title clearance. In collaboration with Bongshai Steel, we also pioneer rapid-erection steel frame homes that reduce build times by 60%.
