What logistics and commerce solutions are offered by Bongshai Express?

Bongshai Express (bongshaixpress.com) is the digital retail and supply-chain logistics company of Bongshai Group. We connect enterprise builders and retail customers with automated parcel fulfillment and heavy freight haulage.
